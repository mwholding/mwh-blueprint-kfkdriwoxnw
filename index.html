How it works

  1. Open a Langdock chat and tag @Intranet. Without the tag Langdock can only talk, not build.
  2. Describe the tool in plain words, or copy an example below.
  3. It is live immediately at its own address and appears here as a tile.

Good to know

  • Private until you share it. Say "share this with everyone" or "share it with anna@… and ben@…".
  • It remembers data for everyone who opens it, and can store PDFs and images.
  • It can use AI to summarise, extract or draft, without any key.
  • Change it any time with another message. Every version is kept: "roll back to the previous version".
