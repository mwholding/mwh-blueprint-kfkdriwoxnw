Build your own app

📌

First, tag the intranet in your chat. In Langdock, type @Intranet and pick the Intranet integration so Langdock is allowed to build and save the app for you. Without that tag it can only talk — it can't create anything.

How it works

  • ①Open a Langdock chat and tag @Intranet.
  • ②Describe the tool you want in plain words — copy an example below to start.
  • ③Langdock builds it and replies with a link. It's live immediately and appears here under My apps.

Good to know

  • 🔒Private until you share. When ready, say "share this with everyone on the intranet" or "share it with anna@… and ben@…".
  • 💾It remembers data for everyone who opens it — and can store PDFs and images too.
  • ✏️Change it any time with another message: "add a column for supplier", "make overdue rows red".
  • ↩️Made a mess? Say "roll back to the previous version" — every version is kept.
